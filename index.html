/ CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Trust starts with
honest promises.

Important principles for a platform handling campaigns, payments and product expectations.

Is Launchglass an investment platform?

No. The initial plan is reward-based crowdfunding. Supporters do not receive company shares, equity or investment returns.

Does a funded campaign guarantee delivery?

No. Product development and manufacturing involve uncertainty. Campaigns must disclose risks, delivery estimates and creator responsibilities.

Are payments live on this website?

No. The uploaded HTML is a frontend demonstration. Live payments require backend integration, provider approval, compliant fund handling and tested refund and payout workflows.

Are AI market findings verified?

Not automatically. AI outputs must identify sourced facts, estimates and assumptions, and creators should verify important business decisions.

Does manufacturer verification guarantee quality?

No. Verification can confirm defined checks were completed, but it cannot guarantee a supplier's performance or the success of a production run.
